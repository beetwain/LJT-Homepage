## About

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML), with a particular interest in the training and evaluation of large language models. I am broadly interested in making AI more robust, reliable, and useful for humans.

- Reinforcement Learning
